Know your rights

Zero FIR

You can file an FIR at any police station, no matter where the incident happened. They must register it and transfer it to the right station.

Workplace & campus harassment

Every workplace must have an Internal Complaints Committee. You can complain in writing and the process must stay confidential.

Digital privacy rights

Sharing your private images without consent is a crime. You can report it online and ask platforms to take the content down.
